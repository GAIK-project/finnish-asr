Listen and compare

Short real recordings, and what each model wrote. Red words are wrong or extra; the count also includes words the model left out. Our model is a Whisper large-v3 fine-tuned on Finnish dialect interviews by the GAIK project. The examples were picked to show where the models differ on dialect speech; every model's full scores, standard Finnish included, are in the repository.
